## Paid Pilot / Design Partner — $2,500–$7,500

- 60–90 days, scoped up front.
- One tenant, plus one connector **or** one document-folder ingest.
- Success metric: **one delivered client-facing evidence packet** — not a demo,
  a real artifact your client sees.
- 50% deposit to start; 100% of the pilot fee is credited to a year-one
  license.
- Never free. A paid pilot means both sides show up, and the pilot **is** the
  implementation — there is no separate implementation fee later.

## Self-Hosted Annual License — flat per tenant

Priced **per tenant, not per learner.** Why: no per-seat metering agent to run,
nothing to phone home (works air-gapped), and procurement gets one flat number
instead of a headcount audit.

## What you will not find here

- **No auto-renewal.** The license renews only if we both agree in writing.
  If you do nothing, you owe nothing.
- **No hidden implementation fees.** The pilot is the implementation.
- **No renewal ratchet.** No "year two is suddenly 40% more" surprise.
- **Your evidence is yours.** It lives in SQLite/Postgres you control, and the
  code converts to Apache-2.0 on 2030-06-11 (per the BUSL-1.1 Change Date) —
  continuity is built into the license, not promised in a sales call.

## What ComplyOS is (and is not)

ComplyOS is a readiness and evidence layer: it ingests messy training records,
normalizes them, flags renewals and gaps, and packages client-facing evidence.
It does not provide legal advice, certification, or compliance determinations —
your qualified people keep the judgment, the sign-off, and the authority.
