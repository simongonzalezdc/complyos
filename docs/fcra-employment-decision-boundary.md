# ComplyOS FCRA boundary

Status: product/legal guardrail, not legal advice.
Owner: product/legal review + sales enablement.
Review cadence: every positioning change and before any people-decision feature.

## Purpose

This FCRA boundary keeps ComplyOS positioned as HR/L&D evidence automation, not background screening or employment eligibility reporting.

## Do not use

Do not use ComplyOS as:

- a background screening product;
- an employment eligibility report;
- a credit, criminal, driving, sanctions, or credential-screening report;
- a tool to rank, approve, reject, discipline, terminate, promote, or compensate people;
- a replacement for HR, legal, or school decision processes.

## Safe product lane

ComplyOS may:

- identify missing or expired training records;
- show evidence provenance and source-system status;
- create audit packets for training-compliance review;
- support human remediation queues.

ComplyOS should not decide what action to take against a person.

## Escalation triggers

Escalate to product/legal review when a customer asks to:

- use reports for hiring or termination;
- rank employees or students;
- gate compensation, promotion, credentials, or opportunity;
- combine training status with discipline, performance, or protected-class data;
- call the output a background report or employment eligibility screen.

## Required controls

- sales enablement language that states the boundary;
- contract language restricting people-decision use;
- product-review gate for future decisioning workflows;
- audit logs showing ComplyOS output is evidence, not the final people decision;
- customer admin education before pilot launch.
