One core, two tracks.

Workday, Cornerstone, SuccessFactors, Canvas, and similar systems all share the same admin grammar: people, roles, assignments, completions, exceptions, approvals, exports, and audit logs. ComplyOS keeps one evidence model and shifts the language by buyer lane.

Track 01

Workforce compliance

For HR, L&D, safety, security, and operations teams that need proof by worker, policy, site, renewal date, manager, and remediation owner.

  • Expired certifications become open compliance gaps with source evidence.
  • Workday, SuccessFactors, Cornerstone, and CSV profiles speak in employees and managers.
  • Outputs avoid automated hiring, discipline, compensation, or background-screening claims.
Track 02

Campus readiness

For universities, schools, clinical programs, bootcamps, and continuing education teams tracking learners across programs, cohorts, courses, and placement requirements.

  • Campus profiles speak in learners, cohorts, programs, prerequisites, and student records.
  • Canvas, Moodle, Blackboard, and D2L Brightspace stay explicit in the connector matrix.
  • School packets separate FERPA/COPPA/accessibility review from product code claims.

The latest work turned the app into a control plane.

The product now documents and exposes privacy, retention, security-evidence, and governance workflows across CLI, API v1, and MCP. The honest boundary is still clear: ComplyOS prepares evidence; lawyers, auditors, and customers approve the claims.

Control stack

Privacy and retention moved from policy text into service workflows.

Operators can create tenant-scoped privacy requests, record controller approval, export or delete subject data, create/release legal holds, configure retention, and dry-run cleanup before applying deletion.

  • Closed privacy cases
  • Terminal raw import rows and decisions
  • Rejected or expired AI proposals
  • Tenant-scoped evidence entries and action logs
Security packet

Security evidence is packetized, not overclaimed.

The packet maps controls to current references and the missing receipts: access reviews, scan output, backup/restore proof, tabletop notes, monitoring, and auditor review.

Governance packet

AI, school, accessibility, and FCRA boundaries travel together.

AI stays proposal-only. School and accessibility reviews are surfaced as procurement inputs. FCRA/background-screening use stays outside the product boundary unless intentionally built.

Not a badge. A receipt machine.

Public copy should say readiness, control mapping, evidence-backed, and designed for auditor/counsel review. It should not say the product has achieved SOC 2, GDPR, FERPA, COPPA, or accessibility status unless reviewed artifacts authorize that exact wording.

  • What exists: code paths, tests, runbooks, packet generators, and review checklists.
  • What still needs paperwork: contracts, DPAs, subprocessors, VPAT/ACR, and auditor receipts.
