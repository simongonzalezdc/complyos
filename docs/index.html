One core, two tracks.

The underlying record model stays the same. The language, examples, and connector defaults shift for the team buying it.

Track 01

Workforce compliance

For HR, safety, security, and operations teams that need proof by worker, policy, site, renewal date, and remediation owner.

  • Expired certifications become open compliance gaps.
  • Workday and CSV profiles speak in employees, managers, and renewals.
  • Audit exports keep the source record and normalized status together.
Track 02

Campus readiness

For universities, bootcamps, clinical programs, and continuing education teams tracking learners across courses, cohorts, and placement requirements.

  • Campus profiles speak in learners, cohorts, programs, and prerequisites.
  • Canvas, Moodle, Blackboard, and D2L Brightspace stay on the roadmap.
  • Reports show gaps without changing the core compliance engine.
